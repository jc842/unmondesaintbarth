# Secret Beaches of St. Barts: Colombier, Saline and Gouverneur

While Saint-Jean is famed for its vibrant beach clubs, Saint-Barthélemy's soul resides in its wild, untouched coastal reserves.

## The Untamed Trio

- **Anse de Colombier**: Accessible only by a scenic 30-minute cliff path from Petite Anse or by boat; renowned for crystal waters, sea turtles, and total tranquility.
- **Grande Saline**: Hidden behind natural coastal dunes and ancient salt flats; powder-soft sand and sweeping surf.
- **Anse du Gouverneur**: A secluded cove with dramatic mountain cliffs and translucent turquoise water.

Preserving these natural beaches is why St. Barts remains untouched by mass tourism.
