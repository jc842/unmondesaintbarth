# Gustavia Harbour Guide: Superyachts, Swedish Heritage & Designer Boutiques

Named after King Gustav III of Sweden, Gustavia is the postcard-perfect harbor town at the center of island life.

## Highlights of Gustavia

1. **The Yacht Basin**: Home to the world's most magnificent sailing and motor yachts during the winter festive season and the St Barths Bucket.
2. **Carré d'Or Luxury Shopping**: Flagship boutiques of premier Parisian haute couture and fine jewelers set in colonial architecture.
3. **Sunset Dining along the Quayside**: Gourmet dining watching classic schooners return to port.

Gustavia pairs historic Swedish-Caribbean architecture with world-class cosmopolitan service.
